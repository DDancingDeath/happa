# Recurring bills

Part of the [owner guide](../owner-guide.md) (one file per page). Hindi: [recurring.hi.md](recurring.hi.md). The codes in brackets are spec IDs in [calculations.md](../calculations.md).

Only the owner sees this page (More › Money › Recurring bills, next to Workers / Salaries); managers and
members never see it or its data. It is loaded only when opened: nothing is read at start, and each
opening reads every bill once (one read per bill).

**Bills.** Add each bill that repeats: a name (Electricity, Water, Shop rent, Internet, Phone, Property
tax …), how often it comes (monthly, every 2, 3 or 6 months, yearly), the day of the month it is due
(1–28), the month the first bill is due, an optional usual amount and an optional note or account /
consumer number. From these the app works out every bill's month and due date (RB-1): electricity every
2 months from January, due on the 10th, is due on 10 Jan, 10 Mar, 10 May and so on. **Edit** changes the
name, due day, usual amount and note; how often it comes and the first month can be changed only until
something is recorded. **Stop this bill** ends it after the last month you choose; what is still to pay,
or a credit, stays.

**Bill amounts.** When a bill comes, tap **Enter bill amount** and enter the amount for its month (RB-2).
Until then the usual amount counts, if you set one; otherwise the bill shows **Amount not entered yet**.
A wrong amount is corrected by entering it again for the same month: the newest one counts. A bill more
than 3 times the last one asks you to check it, and **Yes, it's right** saves it.

**Payments.** Tap **Record payment**: the amount (the amount still to pay is filled in), which bill it is
for, the date, cash or online and an optional note. A bill can be paid in parts (RB-3). A wrong payment
can be removed (it asks first).

**Each bill** is **Paid**, **Part-paid, ₹X of ₹Y**, **₹Y due**, **Overdue** (its due date has passed and
something is still to pay, or something from an earlier bill is still unpaid) or **Amount not entered
yet** (RB-6, RB-7). Y is the bill plus anything still owed from before (RB-4). What is left carries to the
next bill (RB-5): "₹X still to pay" when less was paid, "Credit ₹X carried to the next bill" when more was
paid. **Bill by bill** lists each bill's month, due date, amount, what was paid, what came from before,
the state and the balance carried on; **Bill amounts and payments** lists every entry. Each row also shows
the next due date (RB-10).

**The cards:** **Due now**, everything still to pay on bills due this month or earlier (RB-8), **Paid
this month**, the money paid this month whatever bill it was for (RB-9), and **Credit carried** (RB-5),
with how many bills are paid, part-paid, due, overdue or without an amount.

**On Today.** When a bill is due within the next 3 days or is overdue, Today shows **Bills due: N, ₹X**
(and how many have no amount entered yet), as last worked out on this phone (RB-11); **Open Recurring
bills** brings you here. It shows once this page was opened on this phone; it reads nothing at start and
is brought up to date once a day.

**Not in Money yet.** Payments recorded here do not change Money › Spending, Today's figures or the cash
drawer; a bill you also enter in Expenses counts only there. The owner has not yet decided how the two
fit. Saving needs the internet.
