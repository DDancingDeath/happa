# Workers / Salaries

Part of the [owner guide](../owner-guide.md) (one file per page). Hindi: [workers.hi.md](workers.hi.md). The codes in brackets are spec IDs in [calculations.md](../calculations.md).

Only the owner sees this page (More › Money › Workers / Salaries); managers and members never see it
or its data. It is loaded only when opened: nothing is read at start, and each opening reads every
worker once (one read per worker).

**Workers.** Add each person on monthly pay with a name, the monthly pay in whole rupees and the month
the pay starts from. **Edit** changes the name, the start month or the pay: a new pay counts from the
month you choose, and earlier months keep the old pay (WP-1). **Mark as left** stops the pay after the
last month you choose; what is still owed, or an advance, stays. **Working again** restarts the last
pay from this month.

**Recording pay.** Open a worker and tap **Record pay or advance**: the amount, Pay or Advance, the
month's pay it counts towards (this month by default), the date it was given, cash or online and an
optional note. A month can have several entries (an advance mid-month, a part, the rest); each counts
towards its month (WP-2). A wrong entry can be removed (it asks first); the month then shows as owed
again.

**Each month** a worker is **Paid**, **Part-paid, ₹X of ₹Y** or **₹Y due** (WP-6). Y is the month's
pay plus anything still owed from earlier months (WP-3), and X what is covered: paid this month plus
any advance carried in (WP-4). What is left carries to the next month (WP-5): "₹X still to pay" when
less was paid, "Advance ₹X carried to next month" when more was paid; that advance pays the next
month's pay first. **Month by month** lists each month's pay, what was paid, the advance used, the
state and the balance carried on; **Payments and advances** lists every entry.

**The cards** for the month shown (‹ › change the month): pay for the month (WP-7), paid out in the month,
the money given in the month whatever month it was for (WP-8), still to pay (WP-9) and advances
carried (WP-10), and how many workers are paid, part-paid and due.

**On Today.** A month's pay counts as due from the 1st of that month: from then on, while someone is
still to be paid, Today shows **Salaries due** with the total and how many workers (WP-11), and
**Open Workers / Salaries** brings you here.

**Not in Money yet.** Pay recorded here does not change Money › Spending, Today or the cash drawer:
the owner has not yet decided how it fits with the labour expense type. Saving needs the internet.
