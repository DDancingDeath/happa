# Today

Part of the [owner guide](../owner-guide.md) (one file per page). Hindi: [today.hi.md](today.hi.md). The codes in brackets are spec IDs in [calculations.md](../calculations.md).

Today's figures, from today's bills, expenses and cash sessions.

The page opens with the net figure for the day (T12) and total in / out (T10, T11). Below it comes what needs attention: the cash drawer prompt, the reminders and, for owners and managers, the Day report. Then the day's figures, the bills and the items. On a computer the reminders and the Day report stand in a column on the right.

| On screen | How it is worked out |
|---|---|
| Purchases, Sales (T1, T2) | Sum of today's bill totals, with the number of bills. |
| Business / personal expenses (T4, T5) | Today's expenses of each kind, cash and online. |
| Cash / online received (T6, T7) | Paid at save on today's sales + due payments received today. |
| Cash / online paid (T8, T9) | Paid at save on today's purchases + today's expenses + due payments paid today. |
| Total in, total out, net (T10–T12) | In = cash + online received; out = cash + online paid; net = in − out. |
| Due received / paid (T13, T14) | Due payments received / paid today. |
| New udhaar today, new to pay today (T15, T16) | What today's own sales / purchases still owe (not the total dues). The all-time totals are on Outstanding. |
| Items (T17) | Today's bill lines by item: kg, value, average rate (value ÷ kg). |
| By bill type, Today's bills (T18, T19) | Each type's total split into cash, online, paid later today, written off and still due; these add up to the total. |
| Reminders: Collect udhaar, Collect wholesale dues, Pay suppliers (REM-1) | Parties with a bill **more than 30 days old** still owing: the headline is their whole dues; "of which overdue (> 30 days)" is the part on those old bills (OVERDUE, owner decision, 8 Nov 2026): Collect udhaar has the same ₹ as Outstanding › Sales › Retail "Overdue (> 30 days)", Pay suppliers as Outstanding › Purchases; Collect wholesale dues follows the Wholesale page's own list. Each party shows its whole due, "₹Y overdue" and the age of its oldest due. |
| Labour: deducted from farmers (T-LAB-1) | Sum of the labour on today's purchase bills. |
| Labour: paid to labourers (T-LAB-4) | Today's business expenses of type Labour (also Labor, Labour charges, Labourers, or Labour and wages written in Hindi; any case): what the labourers were paid. Already in today's business expenses; shown here to compare. |
| Paid more / less than deducted (T-LAB-5) | Paid − deducted: the shop's own labour cost for the day. Shown only; profit does not change (owner decision, 8 Nov 2026). |
| Labour: to pay, estimate (T-LAB-2) | Today's purchase kg ÷ the package weight (Settings › Aadhat, default 35 kg), **rounded up**, × the labour rate. |
| The estimate is more / less than deducted (T-LAB-3) | The gap between the two. Not an error: it happens when labour was unticked on a bill or packets were light. |
| Reminders: To do (REM-5) | Your own reminders ("Collect from Ramesh", a date, a party). You and managers add them with **＋ Add reminder**; everyone sees the ones due today or late and the next 7 days, and anyone may tap **✓ Done** (it goes for everyone). **✎ Change** and **✕ Cancel** are for you and managers. They show in the app only, no phone notification. |
| Repeating reminders (REM-6) | A reminder can repeat every day, week or month. When someone taps **✓ Done**, the next one is made at once, one period later (a late one moves to today or the next date after it, never several late copies); a monthly one keeps its day of the month (the 31st falls on the last day of shorter months). Set "Once" with **✎ Change** to stop it repeating. |
| Reminders: Salaries due (WP-11) | Owner only. A month's pay counts as due from the 1st of that month, so from the 1st it shows the workers still to be paid and the total left (the Workers / Salaries page's "Still to pay" for this month), as last worked out on this phone. **Open Workers / Salaries** opens the page. It shows once Workers / Salaries was opened on this phone; it reads nothing at start and is brought up to date once a day. |
| Reminders: Bills due (RB-11) | Owner only. When a recurring bill is due within the next 3 days or is overdue, it shows **Bills due: N, ₹X** (and how many have no amount entered yet), as last worked out on this phone. **Open Recurring bills** opens the page. It shows once Recurring bills was opened on this phone; it reads nothing at start and is brought up to date once a day. |
