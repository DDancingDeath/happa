happa: computer printing test

A test page for printing from a Windows computer to the shop's Bluetooth receipt printer. It isn't part of the app and saves nothing.

2. Print over Bluetooth (Web Serial)

Pair the printer in Windows Bluetooth settings first. Then choose it here: it may be listed by name or as a COM port.

3. Print with Windows (if step 2 fails)

Needs the printer installed in Windows with its driver (Settings › Printers). Choose it in the print window, paper 58 mm, no margins.
