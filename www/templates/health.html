Automatic rollback

If a check keeps failing on a phone, that phone can switch the feature to its safe behaviour. "Report only" just reports it.

billChecksBlocking with "telemetryOnly": false — on a phone where it trips, the owner and managers are no longer asked for a reason: a block they could override becomes a warning and is saved as an "auto-rollback" override (and in the audit log). Other members are still blocked. Hard blocks (payment split, name for Due, bad rate/quantity) always stop the save.
