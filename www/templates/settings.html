Voice billing

Off unless you switch it on. Tap "Speak the line" on Billing and say the item, weight and rate, e.g. "10 kg rice 30 rupees"; join several lines with "and", and say "customer" before a name. You see what was understood (and hear it, unless muted) before anything is filled: one line fills the form, several are added to the bill only after OK. Voice never saves. The phone's or browser's speech service turns speech into text (usually Google's; it may need the internet); the app keeps no recording or text. Saved on this device.

Crash reports

Off unless you switch it on. Then, if the app crashes or stops responding, Google (Firebase Crashlytics) gets the error and its message, the phone model, the Android and app version, the phone's state (free memory and storage, whether it is rooted) and an install number; about once a day the app also checks Crashlytics' settings with Google, sending the phone details and install number. The app adds no names, bills or amounts, but an error message can include text the app was working on. Google keeps a report 90 days. Saved on this phone.
